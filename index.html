Green Moon prospecting

Field guide

Root pearls grow beneath the forest floor. The husk protects a pink membrane over the orange carrom lobe.

Blade
Follow the husk’s dark crease. Extend the opening if the lip still holds the flap.
Diffuser
Sweep across the exposed pink tissue. A wet patch shines and lifts at the edge.
Grip
Take the wet corner and pull back along the fold. Ease off if it catches. A second finger on the husk can steady it.

This bench ends at the exposed carrom lobe.
