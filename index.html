    Green Moon prospecting

    Field guide

    Root pearls grow beneath the forest floor. The husk protects a pink membrane over the orange carrom lobe.

    Tool feedback
    Blade
    Follow the husk’s dark crease. Extend the opening if the lip still holds the flap.
    Diffuser
    Wet the exposed fold. The glaze fades as it dries, so refresh it if the tissue looks matte.
    Grip
    Follow the raised curl and ease off if it bunches. Wet the next fold before you grip it. Extend the slit if the husk holds.
    Carrom blisters
    Wait for each blister to settle and expose its pale fold. Follow the angle of that fold with the blade. Grip the loosened blister and drag it clear of the opening.
    Fazer and work light
    Hold or sweep the spray slowly until the goop turns green, then rub it off with Wipe. Treat any amber patches that show through and wipe again. Roll the clean pearl under the light to see any acid clouding.
    Case
    Lift the pearl into the padded recess. Seat it flat before closing the case.
    Stone
    Draw the blade along the sharpening stone if it starts bunching the husk.
